How it works

  • A sensor on the real wheel measures the hamster's running; finished 10-minute rounds are settled on Sepolia in batches.
  • Live mode: your call locks against the latest settled batch and resolves the moment the next settlement lands on-chain. Settlements only happen when the hamster runs — a sleeping hamster means a longer wait.
  • Blitz mode: replays real past settlements one by one, so you can play at full speed even while the hamster naps.
  • All numbers on this page come live from api.squeekchain.com. Nothing is simulated.
